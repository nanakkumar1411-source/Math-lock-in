LESSON 1

What a number means in an equation

In a linear equation, the plain number is the value when the variable is 0. The number next to the variable is the change for each 1 more.

A2 = 10d + 471
d = extra hours the satellite kept climbing
471 → the altitude when d = 0, the moment it began to climb
10 → the kilometers gained for each extra hour
TrapPicking "the altitude at a time of d hours." That describes the whole expression 10d + 471, not the 471 by itself. (Practice 3, Q21)

Exponentials work the same way

For f(x) = ax + b, a y-intercept means x = 0, and any number to the power 0 is 1.

y-intercept (0, −323) → f(0) = −323
a0 + b = −323 → 1 + b = −323
b = −324
Your missPractice 1 Advanced Math Q19 was left blank. It only needed the y-intercept and a0 = 1. The x-intercept was extra information.
Two questions to ask"What is happening when the variable is 0?" and "What changes for each 1 more?"
LESSON 2

Rate versus total: graphs and linear models

The height of a graph is the total. The slope is the rate. A flat line means the rate is 0.

rate = change in ychange in x
What you seeWhat it means
Line going upThe total is increasing. The rate is positive.
Straight lineThe rate is constant, even if the total keeps rising.
Flat segmentThe total is not changing. The rate is 0.
Steeper segmentA faster rate.
Trap"The rate increased between x = 0 and x = 4." The line rose, but it was straight, so the rate stayed the same. The total grew. (Practice 3, Q15)

Linear model with a gap in the years

57 thousand in 1991, 224 thousand in 2011, x thousand in 2015.

rate = 224 − 572011 − 1991 = 16720 = 8.35 thousand per year
2011 → 2015 is only 4 years: 8.35 × 4 = 33.4
224 + 33.4 = 257.4 → x ≈ 257
Adding the whole 167 to 224 gives 391, which uses the wrong time span.
Your missPractice 1 Algebra Q15: you answered 391, which is 224 + 167. Always use the rate times the years that actually pass.
LESSON 3

Solve, then plug back in

Before you click, put your answer back into the question and check that it makes sense.

j(x) = mx + 144 and j(12) = 18
18 = 12m + 144 → −126 = 12m → m = −10.5
j(10) = (−10.5)(10) + 144 = −105 + 144 = 39

A second check that takes 5 seconds: the size test

The slope is −10.5, so each step down in x raises j by 10.5. Going from x = 12 to x = 10 is 2 steps, so j should rise by 21: 18 + 21 = 39. An answer like 139 would mean a rise of 121, which can't be right.

Your missPractice 1 Algebra Q19: you answered 139 and the correct answer is 39. The setup was right, so a size check would have caught it.
Desmos checkType y = −10.5x + 144 and look at the table for x = 10.
LESSON 4

Percent: turn everything into multipliers

Increase by p% means multiply by 1 + p/100. Decrease by p% means multiply by 1 − p/100.

ChangeMultiplier
+13%1.13
−16%0.84
+167%2.67
−25%0.75

Three rules

  1. "Times" to "percent greater": subtract 1. "1.13 times 100" means 13% greater than 100.
  2. Back-to-back changes: multiply the multipliers. Never add or subtract the percents.
  3. Working backward: divide by the multiplier. Never subtract the percent.
+167% then −16%: 2.67 × 0.84 = 2.2428
2.2428 − 1 = 1.2428 → net increase of 124.28%
167 − 16 = 151% is the trap answer.
After +25% the price is $80: 80 ÷ 1.25 = 64
80 − 25% of 80 = 60 is the trap answer.
Your missesPractice 1 Q9 ("1.13 times 100") and Practice 1 Q22 (167% then −16%, left blank). Both are one-line multiplier problems.
LESSON 5

Turning words into an equation

Name each group, write how many are in it, then add them to equal the total. First decide what the total counts: sides, dollars or centimeters.

A 30-sided polygon has sides of 3 cm, 4 cm and 8 cm. There are n sides of 3 cm, 6 sides of 4 cm, and 5 times as many 8 cm sides as 3 cm sides.

3 cm sides: n
4 cm sides: 6
8 cm sides: 5n ("5 times as many as n")
n + 6 + 5n = 30 → 6n + 6 = 30
TrapWriting 8(5n) + 3n + 4(6) = 30. That multiplies each count by its length, so 30 would be the perimeter in cm. The question says 30 sides, so you add the counts.
Your missPractice 1 Algebra Q22: you chose that perimeter-style equation.
LESSON 6

Systems: one solution, none, or infinitely many

LinesSolutionsHow to spot it
CrossOneDifferent slopes
ParallelNoneSame slope, different y-intercept. Left sides are multiples, right sides are not.
Same lineInfinitely manyOne equation is a multiple of the other, left side and right side.
8x + 7y = 9
24x + 21y = 27
Divide the second equation by 3 and you get the first. It's the same line.
So every point on 8x + 7y = 9 works. Solve for y with x = r:
y = −8r7 + 97 → point (r, −8r7 + 97)
Your missPractice 1 Algebra Q21: you chose a point built as if the lines were different.
Desmos checkGraph both equations. If you only see one line, they're the same line.
LESSON 7

Inequalities with a negative number

When you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.

−2x + 3 > 11
−2x > 8
x < −4  (flipped)

Working backward to find a constant

If x ≥ −8 is every solution of ax − 24 ≤ 32, what is the greatest possible value of a?

ax ≤ 56
If a were positive: x ≤ 56a (an upper limit, but the answer has a lower limit)
So a is negative and the sign flips: x ≥ 56a
56a = −8 → 56 = −8a → a = −7
Check: −7x − 24 ≤ 32 → −7x ≤ 56 → x ≥ −8 ✓
With a = −1 you get x ≥ −56, which doesn't match.
Your missPractice 3 Q20: you knew the idea and answered −1. "Greatest possible" here doesn't mean pick the biggest-looking negative. Only one value of a works, so the check step finds it.
LESSON 8

Treat an expression as one chunk

When the question asks for the value of an expression and not for x, don't solve for x. Rearrange the equation until the expression sits alone.

3x2 − 18x − 15 = 0, find x2 − 6x
Divide by 3: x2 − 6x − 5 = 0
Add 5: x2 − 6x = 5
Your missPractice 1 Advanced Math Q21: you entered 1534, which looks like a late-module guess. It's a two-step problem.
LESSON 9

Scaling in geometry, and arc length

A radius appears squared in area and volume formulas, so changing the radius changes the answer by the square of the change.

Cylinder: V = πr2h
A second cylinder has R = 7r and H = 8h:
72 × 8 = 49 × 8 = 392 times as large
R = 8r, H = 7h gives 82 × 7 = 448 instead.
Your missPractice 1 Geometry Q20: you chose R = 8r, H = 49h, which squares the height by mistake. Only the radius is squared.

Arc length

s = rθ with θ in radians. A 60° angle is π/3 radians.

r = 3, θ = π3 → s = 3 · π3 = π
LESSON 10

Mean from a frequency table

The mean sits where the data balances. More weight on the high values pulls it up, and symmetric frequencies put it exactly in the middle.

Value708090100Mean
Set A4567above 85
Set C7667exactly 85 (symmetric)

Set A has more data at 90 and 100 than at 80 and 70, so its mean is above the middle, 85. No arithmetic needed.

Your missPractice 1 Q18: you chose Set C, the symmetric one, instead of the one weighted toward the high values.
LESSON 11

Distributing a minus sign

A minus sign in front of parentheses flips every sign inside.

180 − (9x − 560)
= 180 − 9x + 560
= 740 − 9x

This came up in your other chats, so it's a habit to build. Draw an arrow from the minus sign to each term inside the parentheses.
